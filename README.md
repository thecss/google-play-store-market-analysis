## 💼 Business Problem
Nova Mobile Studios develops everyday mobile applications and generates most of its revenue through in-app advertising, making high download volumes essential for business success.

The Head of Product must decide what type of app the company should build next before investing development time and resources. However, without data-driven insights into market demand, user satisfaction, app size, and pricing strategies, selecting the next app presents a significant business risk.

The analytics team needs to provide data-driven recommendations on the best app category, optimal app size, and pricing strategy to support the Head of Product's decision.

<br>

## 🎯 Objective
As a Data Analyst on the Analytics team, I was tasked with performing an exploratory data analysis (EDA).

The analysis focused on answering three key business questions:
1. Which app category has high download demand but low user ratings, indicating an opportunity for improvement?
2. Does app size influence download volume, and what is the optimal app size?
3. Should the next app be free or paid to maximize business value?
   
<br>

## 🗂️ Dataset
* Source: Google Play Store Apps Dataset (Kaggle)
* Size: ~10,840 Android applications
* Key Columns: Category, Rating, Reviews, Installs, Type, Size
